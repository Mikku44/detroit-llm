Detroit LLM Gateway (the **"Service"**) provides web chat and an OpenAI-compatible API (`/v1/chat/completions`, `/v1/models`) across multiple models in one place, operated by **MIKKUCN** (chat.khain.app). By signing up or using the Service, you accept these Terms.

## 1. Service and models

You can switch models per task, e.g. **deepseek-v4-pro / deepseek-v4-flash**, **qwen3.7-flash / qwen3.8-flash** (Alibaba DashScope), **glm-5.3 / glm-5.3-flash / glm-4.5-air / glm-4.7-flashx** (Z.AI), **gemini-2.5-flash**, **Claude Extra** models (Anthropic), and image models (z-image-turbo, glm-image, grok-imagine-image). The live list is at `GET /v1/models` or the **Models** page. Models and availability may change without prior notice.

## 2. Accounts and access

- Sign in with **Google (OAuth)**. The system auto-verifies **YouTube Membership**, or you can subscribe to a paid plan via **Stripe**.
- Keep your **session** and **API keys** (`sk-dt-...`) secret. Do not share them or embed them in public client-side code. You are responsible for damage from leaked keys (you can revoke and reissue keys on the **Keys** page).
- The **Free** plan is available without payment within the free-tier quota.

## 3. Plans, quotas, and technical limits

- Quotas are token-based per **day/week/month** (input + output combined) according to your tier, e.g. **Free is 100,000 tokens/week and 435,000 tokens/month** (daily = weekly / 7). Paid plans (**Nomad / Dreamer / Entrepreneur / Angel** and **Extra Claude** packs) have higher quotas as shown on the **Upgrade/Usage** pages.
- **Free-tier models:** the `flash` family (e.g. deepseek-v4-flash, qwen3.7-flash, qwen3.8-flash, gemini-2.5-flash) plus glm-4.5-air / glm-4.7-flashx, except **deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp, glm-5.3 and glm-5.3-flash** which require membership/paid plans. **Claude Extra** models require a direct Extra Claude pack with separate input/output quotas and a limit of 2 concurrent requests.
- Image generation has a separate monthly quota (e.g. **2 images/month on Free**) per tier.
- Rate limit is **60 requests/minute** with a **~1MB** request cap. Exceeding quotas returns a temporary **403/429** and is not a service outage.
