Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use the AI Assistant service (DeepSeek V4 Flash) for YouTube Membership subscribers.

1. Information We Collect
To provide the service efficiently and verify access, we may collect the following data:

Account information: Such as YouTube Channel ID, Discord User ID, or Email used to verify your membership status.

Google User Data / YouTube API Data will be used strictly for membership authentication and will not be shared with or transferred to third parties.

Usage data: Number of messages sent, timestamps, prompt length, and quota consumption history.

Chat history/logs: The questions (input prompts) you send to the system for processing.

2. How We Use Your Data
We use your data only for the following purposes:

Access verification: Verify your YouTube Membership status to grant access at your membership tier.

System administration: Control usage volume according to the Fair Use Policy (e.g., daily quota counting and anti-spam).

Service improvement: Debug system errors and improve the performance of the Bot/Web App.

3. Third-Party Processing
DeepSeek API: Your prompts are sent to the DeepSeek API for processing to generate responses. Data is handled according to DeepSeek's privacy policy and security standards.

No data selling: We never sell or exchange your personal data or chat history to third parties for commercial gain.

4. Data Security
We use appropriate technical measures to protect personal data and API keys from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.

Identity information is used only to verify membership, and we do not store financial or credit card data (payments are handled entirely through YouTube).

5. Data Storage
We store your data securely on our own servers, under the following principles:

- Chat history: Your conversations are stored in a separate database from your account information. For paid members, message content is encrypted (at-rest encryption) so that only the user can read it back.
- Retention period: We keep your chat history so you can return to it or continue using it. You may delete your chat history or stored data at any time by requesting it through our support channel.
- Backups: We may back up data for system safety; such backups are protected with the same security measures.

6. Your Rights
You may disconnect your account or stop using the service at any time; your access will end when your YouTube Membership status ends.

You may request deletion of your chat history or stored data by contacting the service provider's support channel.

This Privacy Policy may be updated as appropriate. Significant changes will be communicated through the service channels.
